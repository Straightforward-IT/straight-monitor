# GVP Collective Bargaining Agreement Reference
**Stand: Januar 2026**  
**Applicable to:** Zeitarbeit (Temporary/Staffing) in Germany  
**Parties:** DGB (Trade Unions) & GVP (Gesamtverband der Personaldienstleister)

---

## Critical Business Rules for Straight Monitor

This document outlines the baseline union contract terms that govern ALL employee compensation, working hours, and employment conditions in our staffing operations. These rules are **non-negotiable defaults** and must be enforced in compensation calculations, scheduling, and compliance workflows.

---

## 1. WORKING HOURS & COMPENSATION

### Base Working Hours
- **Full-Time:** 151.67 hours/month = 35 hours/week average
- **Alternative:** Up to 173.34 hours/month (40 hrs/week) if explicitly contracted and justified by customer requirements
- **Part-Time:** Prorated based on contract; same rights/duties as full-time within their scheduled hours

### Hours Calculation Key Points
- Umkleiden (changing clothes), washing, and breaks (breakfast, lunch, coffee) = **NOT counted as work time**
- Exceptions: Only if customer site has different rules (apply customer rules in that case)
- Partial shift cycles (e.g., Contischicht) = use monthly average, not pro-rata per cycle

### Holidays (§ 3.6)
- **Christmas Eve (24.12) & New Year's Eve (31.12):** Work ends at 14:00
  - Work after 14:00 = holiday premium (100%)
  - Day can be offset via time-off account or vacation

---

## 2. OVERTIME & PREMIUMS (§ 6)

### Overtime (Mehrarbeit)
- **Trigger:** Hours exceed individualized monthly target by **>15%**
  - Example: 151.67 hrs base → premium kicks in after 174.42 hrs
- **Premium Rate:** 25% of hourly tariff rate
- **Calculation:** Include vacation hours in threshold calculation (not just actual worked hours)

### Night Work (23:00–06:00)
- **Premium:** Up to 25% (customer site rules may differ; use customer if lower)

### Sunday & Holiday Work (Sonn- und Feiertagsarbeit)
- **Sunday:** Up to 50% premium
- **Legal Holiday:** Up to 100% premium
- **Christmas Eve / New Year's Eve (after 14:00):** 100% premium
- **Precedence Rule:** If multiple premiums apply, pay only the **highest** rate

### Premium Payment Rules
- Premiums paid with monthly salary **in the month they occur**
- Premiums are **NOT transferred to time-off account**
- Time-off account payouts only use base tariff rate (§ 4.7)

---

## 3. TIME-OFF ACCOUNT (Arbeitszeitkonto) (§ 4)

### Purpose
Reconcile monthly variance between contracted hours and actual customer-site hours.

### Limits & Safeguards
| Scenario | Limit | Escalation |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| Regular Plus Hours | 200 hours | Must insolvency-protect over 150 hrs |
| Seasonal Variance | 230 hours | Must insolvency-protect over 150 hrs |
| Carryover to Next Year | 150 hours max | Over 150 hrs → must pay out in cash |
| Part-Time | Prorated | Insolvency protection still at 150 hrs threshold |

### Reconciliation Cycle
- **Standard:** 12-month balance
- **Grace Period:** If imbalance remains after 12 months → employer has 3 more months to arrange
- **Overflow Rules:**
  - If still unbalanced after 15 months → up to 150 hrs can roll to next year
  - Anything beyond 150 hrs → **must be paid out in cash**

### Time-Off Withdrawal (§ 4.4)
Employee can request time-off for plus-hours under these conditions:

| Condition | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Per 35 Plus Hours** | 1 work day off (during active customer assignment) |
| **Frequency Limit** | Max 1x per calendar month for up to 2 days |
| **Notice Required** | 1 week advance notice |
| **Employer Override** | Can reject for "urgent operational reasons" with mandatory make-up scheduling |
| **Self-Pay Threshold** | 91+ plus hours → employee can demand cash payment |

### Special Cases
- Illness during approved time-off → hours revert to account (§ 4.4g)
- Can negotiate up to 20 hrs/month payout + up to 70 hrs per balancing cycle as cash (§ 4.4)

## 4. VACATION (Urlaub) (§ 9)

### Annual Entitlement
| Tenure | Days/Year |
|--------|-----------|
| Year 1 | 25 days |
| Year 2–3 | 27 days |
| Year 4+ | 30 days |

### Calculation & Rules
- **Minimum Reference Period:** Last 3 months of employment
- **Carryover:** Unused vacation must be taken by **31 March** of following year or it expires
  - **Exception:** If long-term illness prevented use → expires but is paid (§ 9.6)
- **Part-Time Adjustment:** Prorated based on days/week worked
- **Partial Year:** 1/12th per full month employed
- **Holiday Overlap:** If legal holiday falls during vacation:
  - Use **customer site's holiday calendar** if on assignment
  - Use **employer's home office holiday calendar** if not assigned

### Scheduling
- Must coordinate with fixed customer assignments (already-approved vacations are unavailable for customer work)

---

## 5. SICK PAY & ENTGELTFORTZAHLUNG (§ 10)

### Calculation Basis
- **Reference Period:** Last 3 complete months before illness start (or vacation start)
- **Base Rate:** Average hourly earnings from reference period
- **Include:** All regular compensation + overtime premiums + bonuses (except Mehrarbeitszuschlag)
- **Averaging Method:**
  - Base on **individualized regular working hours** (not actual hours)
  - Add proportional premiums earned above regular hours
  - Use reference period averages for fluctuating pay

### Kurzarbeit (Short-time Work)
- If reference period included short-time → separate calculation rules apply
- Must adjust for reduced-hour periods in averaging

---

## 6. TERMINATION & NOTICE PERIODS (§ 2.4)

### Standard Termination
- **By law:** 4 weeks to 15th or end of calendar month (§ 622 BGB)
- **Part-time doesn't change this**

### Probation (First 3 Months)
- **Either party:** 1 week notice
- **New hire restart:** After 3-month gap, probation resets (§ 2.4 Abs. 2)

### Extended Probation (Months 3–6)
- **Either party:** 2 weeks notice

### Bonus Transitions
- New tariff (DGB/GVP) became effective: Jan 2026
- **Legacy BAP-contracts:** Special transition rules apply until **31.12.2027**
  - Allows up to 2-year contract extensions (4x renewal cycles) without just cause until Dec 31, 2027
  - After 2027, standard law applies

---

## 7. RETIREMENT & CONTRACT END (§ 2.3)

### Automatic Termination
- Employment **automatically ends** when employee reaches full statutory retirement age (Regelaltersrente)
- Does **not apply** to contracts signed before 01.01.2026 under old BAP terms
- Individual employment agreements can override

## 9. TRAVEL COMPENSATION (§ 7.3, 7.4)

### Long Commute Premium (Wegezeit)
- **Threshold:** >1 hour 15 minutes one-way using fastest public transit
- **Compensation:** Full tariff rate for time **over 1:15** (both directions)
- **Claim Process:**
  - Employee must notify in text form by month-end
  - For ongoing assignments: report once + notify only of changes
  - Must prove actual commute time used

### Overnight Travel (>2 hours commute)
- **Accommodation Cost:** Employer covers full cost (or negotiates flat rate per tax law)
- **Employer Organization:** Typically employer arranges lodging
- **Employee Self-Book:** Requires pre-approval + receipt for reimbursement

### Other Expense Reimbursement (§ 7.5)
- Must be defined individually per contract (§ 670 BGB)

## 10. DISPUTE & PROCEDURE (Verfahrensvereinbarung)

### Escalation Path
- **Level 1:** Direct discussion between parties
- **Level 2:** If unresolved → written notice + meeting within defined timeframe
- **Level 3:** Arbitration/Joint Commission per DGB-GVP procedure (see page 32+)

### Exclusion Clauses (Ausschlussfristen)
- Claims must be asserted **within defined periods**
- Details on page 17 (§ 13)

### Strike Clause (Streikklausel) (§ 15, page 19)
- Standard union strike protections apply
- Review full text for exemptions

## 11. TRANSITION RULES (CRITICAL FOR 2026–2029)

### For Companies Transitioning FROM iGZ Contracts
**Until 31.12.2029:**
- Can continue variable hours model (based on workdays/month)
- Overtime thresholds differ:
  | Workdays/Month | Overtime Trigger |
  |---|---|
  | 20 | After 160 hrs |
  | 21 | After 168 hrs |
  | 22 | After 176 hrs |
  | 23 | After 184 hrs |
- **Once-only switch:** Employer can convert to fixed hours model (151.67/month) any time until 31.12.2029
- After 2029: Must use standard DGB/GVP model

### For Companies Transitioning FROM BAP Contracts
**Until 31.12.2027:**
- Can use extended probation rules (2-year fixed term + 4 renewals)
- Retirement rules don't apply to pre-2026 contracts
- After 2027: Standard law applies

## 12. IMPLEMENTATION CHECKLIST FOR ENGINEERING

### Compensation Engine
- [ ] Base hourly rate from Entgeltgruppe (salary group § 3)
- [ ] Apply experience surcharge if 6+ months tenure (§ 3)
- [ ] Apply industry-specific surcharge if applicable (§ 4)
- [ ] Calculate overtime threshold = 115% of monthly contracted hours
- [ ] Identify all work premiums (night, holiday, Sunday) per customer rules
- [ ] Apply only **highest** premium if multiple overlap
- [ ] Segregate premiums from time-off account (no carryover)
- [ ] Track plus/minus hours monthly for reconciliation

### Time-Off Account Management
- [ ] Cap plus hours at 200 (230 for seasonal; 150 for part-time prorated)
- [ ] Flag for insolvency protection when >150 hrs
- [ ] Auto-reconcile after 12 months with 3-month grace
- [ ] Enforce 150-hr carryover limit + cash payout for overflow
- [ ] Track withdrawal requests (1 week notice, max 2 days/month, 35 hrs per day)
- [ ] Revert hours if employee ill during approved time-off

### Vacation Management
- [ ] Entitle 25/27/30 days based on tenure (prorated for partial years)
- [ ] Track carryover with 31 March expiration (except medical exceptions)
- [ ] Block vacation days from customer assignment calendar
- [ ] Use customer-site holiday calendar if on assignment

### Payroll Compliance
- [ ] Reference last 3 months for sick pay & vacation calculations
- [ ] Include all bonuses except Mehrarbeitszuschlag in averaging
- [ ] Prorate for Kurzarbeit or reduced-hour periods
- [ ] Pay premiums/bonuses in the month they occur
- [ ] Distinguish between time-off rate (base only) and premium rates

### Termination & Probation
- [ ] Track start date for probation reset logic
- [ ] Enforce 1-week notice during first 3 months
- [ ] Enforce 2-week notice for months 3–6
- [ ] Check contract date: apply transition rules if pre-2026 (BAP) or iGZ-bound

### Transition Logic
- [ ] Detect legacy BAP/iGZ contracts and apply correct ruleset until 2027/2029
- [ ] Offer one-time conversion option for iGZ companies before 2029 year-end

## Key Compliance Risks

🚨 **High Risk Areas:**
1. **Overtime Threshold Miscalculation** → Wage theft exposure
2. **Misclassifying Hours as Break Time** → Compensation underpayment
3. **Incorrect Premium Stacking** → Overpayment or underpayment
4. **Time-Off Account Insolvency Risk** → Legal liability (>150 hrs must be protected)
5. **Vacation Carryover Violations** → Employee claims after 31 March
6. **Transition Rule Lapses** → Wrong rules applied post-2027/2029
7. **Commute Calculation Errors** → Disputed reimbursements
